# Pinterest Strategy

## ROLE

Pinterest is an evergreen visual discovery and search channel.

Do not treat Pinterest like Instagram.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Drive qualified traffic to the LMG website.

---

# BEST CONTENT

Prioritize:

* Recipes
* Wellness checklists
* Guides
* Infographics
* Healthy meal ideas
* Menopause resources
* Healthy ageing
* Sleep
* Gut health

---

# PIN STRUCTURE

Every pin should have:

1. Clear title
2. Strong visual
3. Search-friendly description
4. Relevant keywords
5. Destination URL

---

# TITLE EXAMPLES

* 7 Simple Habits for Better Sleep
* Healthy Ageing Checklist
* 10 High-Fibre Foods to Add to Your Diet
* Menopause Wellness Guide
* Easy Healthy Breakfast Ideas

---

# PINTEREST PRINCIPLE

Prioritize evergreen content.

A useful pin should continue generating traffic weeks or months after publication.

---

# PRODUCT PINS

Product-related content should still educate.

Example:

> What to Look for When Choosing a Magnesium Supplement

Then connect to relevant products.

---

# MEASUREMENT

Track:

* Impressions
* Saves
* Outbound clicks
* Website sessions
* Email signups
* Product views
* Purchases
