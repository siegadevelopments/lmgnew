# Instagram Strategy

## ROLE

Instagram is LMG's visual discovery, community and brand-building channel.

---

# PRIORITY

1. Reels
2. Carousels
3. Stories
4. Static posts

---

# REELS

Use for:

* Education
* Expert clips
* Vendor interviews
* Recipes
* Wellness tips
* Myth vs fact

---

# CAROUSELS

Use for:

* Lists
* Checklists
* Educational explanations
* Step-by-step guides
* Comparisons

---

# STORIES

Use for:

* Polls
* Questions
* Behind the scenes
* New articles
* Vendor features
* Product discovery
* Community interaction

---

# CAROUSEL STRUCTURE

Slide 1:

Strong hook.

Slide 2:

Problem.

Slides 3–7:

Useful information.

Final slide:

CTA.

---

# REEL STRUCTURE

Hook
→ Problem
→ Explanation
→ Practical advice
→ CTA

---

# INSTAGRAM RULE

Optimize for:

* Saves
* Shares
* Comments
* Watch time
* Profile visits
* Website clicks

Followers are a secondary metric.
