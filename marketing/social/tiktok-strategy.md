# TikTok Strategy

## ROLE

TikTok is a discovery and search-driven education platform.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Reach people who are actively interested in wellness questions.

---

# FORMAT REQUIREMENT — NATIVE VIDEO ONLY

Every TikTok post must be a real filmed (or screen-recorded) video.

Never publish a static image or a slideshow of a single image as a TikTok post.

TikTok's algorithm ranks primarily on watch time and completion rate — a still image cannot generate either signal, so it will not get meaningful reach even if the platform technically accepts it.

If a real video isn't ready yet, the caption/brief should stay in Draft rather than be scheduled.

---

# VIDEO LENGTH

Sweet spot for educational content: **30–60 seconds**, with **42–54 seconds** as the ideal — enough time to state the problem, explain it, and land the takeaway without rushing.

Shorter (11–18s) suits pure hook/punchline content. Longer (60–90s) is fine only if a topic genuinely needs it and every second earns its place — don't pad runtime.

TikTok's algorithm now rewards completion rate over raw view count, so the right length is whichever one this specific topic can hold attention for, not simply "as short as possible."

---

# VIDEO STRUCTURE

0–1 second:

ON-SCREEN TEXT HOOK (must work with sound off — see VISUAL RULES)

1–10 seconds:

Spoken HOOK + PROBLEM

10–45 seconds:

EDUCATION

45–55 seconds:

ACTIONABLE TIP

55–60 seconds:

CTA

---

# SEARCH-DRIVEN CONTENT

Build videos around real questions.

Examples:

* How to improve gut health
* How to sleep better
* What happens during menopause
* How to support healthy ageing
* What causes afternoon energy crashes

## TikTok SEO — caption keywords

TikTok's search/recommendation system reads captions, on-screen text and spoken audio, not just hashtags.

* Lead the caption with the topic/keyword in the first 50 characters (this is what's visible before the "more" cut-off).
* Say the keyword out loud AND show it on-screen — don't rely on hashtags alone to signal topic.
* Mention the keyword naturally once or twice. Repeating it multiple times reads as spammy and doesn't help ranking.

---

# VISUAL RULES

Use:

* Human faces
* Clear captions (burned-in subtitles — most viewers watch muted)
* On-screen keywords
* Good lighting
* Clear audio
* Fast but natural pacing

Hook text overlay specifics:

* Must appear within the first second.
* Under 10 words.
* High contrast (white text/black outline or black text/white outline), sans-serif font.
* Positioned upper-centre of frame so it isn't covered by TikTok's UI.
* One idea per overlay — don't stack multiple text elements on screen at once.

Test rule: watch the first 3 seconds on mute. If the topic/problem/promise isn't clear from the visual + on-screen text alone, the hook is too slow.

Avoid excessive effects.

---

# CONTENT TYPES

Prioritize:

* Expert answers
* Wellness tips
* Myth vs fact
* Recipes
* Vendor interviews
* Founder stories
* Product education
* Q&A

---

# CTA

TikTok does not make links in captions clickable — a caption URL is plain, non-tappable text. Never write out a literal URL in a TikTok caption or brief.

Direct people to the bio link instead (see `cta-library.md` → TIKTOK section).

Prefer:

> Follow for practical wellness education.

> Link in bio for the full guide on Lifestyle Medicine Gateway.

> Save this for later.

Avoid aggressively selling in every video.

---

# POSTING CADENCE

Aim for **3–5 posts per week**.

One genuinely useful, well-made video consistently outperforms several rushed daily posts — prioritise quality and a sustainable cadence over hitting a daily quota.
