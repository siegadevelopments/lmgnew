# TikTok Strategy

## ROLE

TikTok is a discovery and search-driven education platform.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Reach people who are actively interested in wellness questions.

---

# VIDEO STRUCTURE

0–3 seconds:

HOOK

3–10 seconds:

PROBLEM

10–45 seconds:

EDUCATION

45–55 seconds:

ACTIONABLE TIP

55–60 seconds:

CTA

---

# SEARCH-DRIVEN CONTENT

Build videos around real questions.

Examples:

* How to improve gut health
* How to sleep better
* What happens during menopause
* How to support healthy ageing
* What causes afternoon energy crashes

---

# VISUAL RULES

Use:

* Human faces
* Clear captions
* On-screen keywords
* Good lighting
* Clear audio
* Fast but natural pacing

Avoid excessive effects.

---

# CONTENT TYPES

Prioritize:

* Expert answers
* Wellness tips
* Myth vs fact
* Recipes
* Vendor interviews
* Founder stories
* Product education
* Q&A

---

# CTA

Prefer:

> Follow for practical wellness education.

> Read the full guide on Lifestyle Medicine Gateway.

> Save this for later.

Avoid aggressively selling in every video.
