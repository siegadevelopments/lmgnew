# Social Media Analytics

## PRIMARY BUSINESS METRICS

Track:

1. Website traffic
2. Email signups
3. Product views
4. Add-to-cart
5. Purchases
6. Revenue
7. Repeat purchases

---

# CONTENT METRICS

Track:

* Reach
* Impressions
* Views
* Average watch time
* Completion rate
* Saves
* Shares
* Comments
* Profile visits
* Link clicks

---

# PLATFORM-SPECIFIC

## TikTok

Prioritize:

* Watch time
* Completion
* Shares
* Search discovery

## Instagram

Prioritize:

* Saves
* Shares
* Reels watch time
* Profile visits

## Facebook

Prioritize:

* Comments
* Shares
* Link clicks
* Community engagement

## Pinterest

Prioritize:

* Saves
* Outbound clicks
* Website sessions

---

# MONTHLY REVIEW

Identify:

### TOP TOPICS

Which wellness topics generated the strongest results?

### TOP HOOKS

Which opening statements performed best?

### TOP FORMATS

Which formats generated the strongest engagement?

### TOP CONVERSION CONTENT

Which content generated website activity and purchases?

---

# DECISION RULE

Do not automatically repeat content simply because it received likes.

Prioritize content that demonstrates:

Reach
+
Engagement
+
Traffic
+
Conversion

---

# EXPERIMENTATION

Every month test:

* New hooks
* New topics
* New formats
* Different video lengths
* Different CTAs
* Different posting times

Record results.
