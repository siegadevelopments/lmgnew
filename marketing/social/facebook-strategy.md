# Facebook Strategy

## ROLE

Facebook is primarily a community and relationship channel.

---

# CONTENT

Use:

* Educational posts
* Questions
* Polls
* Recipes
* Articles
* Videos
* Vendor stories
* Community discussions

---

# DISCUSSION STRATEGY

Do not simply publish information.

Ask questions.

Examples:

> What's one healthy habit you've successfully maintained?

> What's harder for you: eating well or getting enough sleep?

> What wellness topic would you like us to cover next?

---

# COMMUNITY PRINCIPLE

Reply to comments.

Continue conversations.

Ask follow-up questions.

Turn useful comments into future content.

---

# FACEBOOK CONTENT STYLE

Facebook can support slightly longer captions than Instagram.

Explain context.

Tell stories.

Create discussion.

---

# OBJECTIVE

Build a community that sees LMG as a trusted wellness resource rather than simply another store.
