# LMG Content Engine

## ROLE

Operate Lifestyle Medicine Gateway's content production system.

---

# STEP 1 — IDENTIFY A PROBLEM

Find a real wellness question.

Examples:

* How can I sleep better?
* What foods support gut health?
* How can I age well?
* What happens during menopause?

---

# STEP 2 — RESEARCH

Before publishing health-related educational content:

* Verify factual claims.
* Prefer authoritative sources.
* Avoid unsupported claims.
* Identify uncertainty.
* Avoid presenting correlation as causation.

---

# STEP 3 — CREATE THE CORE ASSET

Usually:

* Article
* Guide
* Recipe
* Interview
* Expert discussion

---

# STEP 4 — CREATE SHORT-FORM CONTENT

Extract:

* 3–5 short video topics
* 3–5 hooks
* 1 carousel
* 3–5 Pinterest ideas
* 1 Facebook discussion

---

# STEP 5 — CONNECT TO BUSINESS

Determine whether the topic naturally connects to:

* Product
* Vendor
* Service
* Email signup
* Related article
* Recipe
* Community

Do not force a commercial connection.

---

# STEP 6 — DISTRIBUTE

Publish platform-native versions.

---

# STEP 7 — MEASURE

Track:

* Reach
* Watch time
* Saves
* Shares
* Comments
* Website traffic
* Email signups
* Product views
* Purchases

---

# STEP 8 — LEARN

Identify:

* Best topics
* Best hooks
* Best formats
* Best platforms
* Best CTAs

---

# STEP 9 — DOUBLE DOWN

Create more content around winning topics.

Do not simply create new content every week.

Expand successful topics.

---

# CONTENT LOOP

Create
→ Publish
→ Measure
→ Learn
→ Improve
→ Repurpose
→ Repeat
