# Content Repurposing System

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Create one strong idea and distribute it across multiple channels.

---

# EXAMPLE

CORE TOPIC:

> How to improve sleep naturally.

---

## WEBSITE

Create:

> How to Improve Sleep Naturally: 9 Practical Strategies

---

## VIDEO

Create:

60-second video:

> 3 things to try tonight if you're struggling with sleep.

---

## INSTAGRAM

Create:

Reel:

> 3 ways to improve sleep.

Carousel:

> 7 habits that may be affecting your sleep.

Story:

> What's your biggest sleep challenge?

---

## TIKTOK

Create:

> Why are you still tired after sleeping 8 hours?

---

## FACEBOOK

Create:

Long-form educational post explaining the topic.

Then ask:

> What's the hardest part of getting good sleep for you?

---

## PINTEREST

Create:

* Sleep checklist
* Evening routine
* Sleep infographic
* Sleep tips

Each should link to the relevant website content.

---

## EMAIL

Subject/topic:

> Are you getting enough restorative sleep?

Link to the article.

---

# REPURPOSING RULE

Never blindly duplicate.

Adapt:

* Hook
* Length
* Format
* CTA
* Visual presentation
* Caption

to the platform.

---

# VENDOR REPURPOSING

One vendor interview can become:

* Full interview
* 5 short videos
* 3 quote graphics
* Vendor article
* Facebook post
* Instagram Reel
* TikTok
* Pinterest graphics
* Email feature
* Product education post
